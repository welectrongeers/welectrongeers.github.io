About this journal

I'm an electrical engineer (BE Electrical, Mumbai University) building a career in the MV switchgear and AIS panel industry. This site is where I write down what I'm learning along the way — interview questions I've actually been asked, technical concepts I had to relearn properly, and honest notes on what it's like breaking into companies like L&T, Siemens, Schneider Electric, ABB, and CG Power.

Most career advice online is generic. This isn't — it's specific to this industry, written by someone currently going through the fresher job search rather than looking back on it from a distance.

If you're a fellow fresher trying to break into switchgear, panel design, or a related field, I hope these notes save you some time.
